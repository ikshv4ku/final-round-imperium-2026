Tilt your phone. This is camera tracking.

With tracking on, the wall redraws the palace from the camera’s viewpoint, so near arches move more than the distant palace and the actor looks truly inside the set. Turn it off and the wall becomes a flat painting. This is what Kamalistaan’s retrofitted soundstages will sell to Orion and others.
